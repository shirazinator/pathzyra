# 🧭 Pathzyra
### *Designed by Shiraz*

> **The Honest, Hyper-Personalized Career & Exam Roadmap Engine.**  
> Built for students and working professionals preparing for competitive exams (SSC CGL, UPSC, CAT, GATE, Banking) and high-impact tech & business roles (SDE, Data Science, DevOps, PM).

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **The "Honest Reality Check" Engine:**  
   Calculates your real-life study capacity ($\text{available hours}$) against benchmark requirements ($\text{benchmark hours}$). If a timeline is unrealistic (e.g. cracking Google SDE or SSC CGL in 2 months studying 1 hour/day), it alerts you with guaranteed consequences and auto-prescribes the exact realistic timeline.

2. **Working Professional vs. Full-Time Aspirant Modes:**  
   Accommodates weekday evening study (1–3 hrs/day) and weekend deep-work surges (4–8 hrs/day).

3. **80/20 Pareto Prioritization:**  
   Tags all tasks into **Tier 1 (High Yield — 70% exam weightage)**, **Tier 2 (Good to have)**, and **Optional**.

4. **100% Free Gold-Standard Resources:**  
   Direct links to renowned open-access courses, playlists, and sheets (Striver A2Z, NeetCode 150, Gate Smashers, Neetu Singh English, Gagan Pratap Maths, Rodha CAT, etc.).

5. **"Life Happened / Recalibrate" Guilt-Free Recovery:**  
   Missed 3 days due to work or life? Recalculates remaining weeks without forcing you to restart from Day 1.

6. **Interactive Checklist & Notion/Markdown Export:**  
   Track weekly tasks with local storage persistence and export to Notion, Obsidian, or print as a physical planner.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Obsidian & Electric Emerald theme)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Celebration:** Canvas Confetti
